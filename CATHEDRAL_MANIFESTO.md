# THE CATHEDRAL: RULE OF LIFE

> "The Master of your own two hands. Today I will build. Tomorrow I will build again."

## Core Philosophy
The Cathedral is not a productivity app; it is a **Formation Engine**. It exists to transform technical skill (TECHNE) into a defense for the truth of history (HISTORIA) and the dignity of free men.

### The Four Pillars
1. **⚙️ TECHNE (The Forge)**: The discipline of building. Deep work blocks aimed at mastery of the stack.
2. **📜 HISTORIA (The Archive)**: The discipline of roots. Engaging with primary sources to understand human nature and the era.
3. **🏛️ GYMNOS (The Arena)**: The discipline of the body. Physical fortitude as the vessel for the soul.
4. **🌙 SOPHIA (The Sanctuary)**: The discipline of reflection. Evening silence, journaling, and philosophical alignment.

---

## The Living Schedule
The schedule is a fixed "Rule of Life" (The Codex) that remains constant in its timing but fluid in its context.

### Weekly Intentions (Week's Rule)
Every Monday, the user declares the **Week's Rule**:
- **Active Project**: What codebase or feature is being forged in Techne.
- **Primary Source**: The specific book or text being mined in Historia.
- **Research Focus**: The active question or person of interest.
- **Body Focus**: The physical emphasis for the week.
- **Evening Theme**: The philosophical lens for reflection.
- **Weekly Aim**: The singular sentence that defines the week's advance.

### Personalization Logic
The app dynamically injects these intentions into the ritual tasks:
- **Deep Work**: "Work on: [Project]" replaces generic "Write code".
- **Primary Source**: "Read from: [Book]" replaces generic "Read section".
- **Peripatetic Walk**: "Turn over: [Research Focus]" guides the phone-less walk.
- **Grounding**: The morning sentence is aimed directly at the **Weekly Aim**.

---

## The Formation Path
Progress is tracked not by "completed tasks" in a list, but by **Rituals Sealed**. 
- **The Score**: Measured by fidelity to the hours.
- **The Streak**: The cumulative power of minor ritual.
- **The Heatmap**: A visual record of stones laid in the Cathedral wall.

---

## Accountability: The 2-Day Rule
A man may stumble, but he must not fall. 
- If two consecutive days pass without discipline, the **Broken Contract** dialog appears.
- It requires a manual re-commitment to the **Purpose Statement**.
- "The walls of your Cathedral are weakening. Are you still committed to the path?"

---

## The Interface
- **Monastery Black**: For focus and depth.
- **Cathedral Gold**: For value and the "Solar" aspect of the work.
- **Parchment**: For the weight of the written word.
- **The Sunflower**: A symbol of fidelity, always turning toward the Light (the Purpose).
